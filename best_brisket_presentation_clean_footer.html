MARKETING

PREPARATION TIMELINE

From raw ingredients to final sale

1Purchasing

Buy meat, buns, sauces, toppings, sides, drinks, smoker chips and packaging. Keep every receipt.

2Day Before

Season meat, prepare cold ingredients and organise equipment.

3Cooking

Smoke brisket and pork, use butcher’s paper as required, rest and portion meat.

4Morning

Pack 300 chilled side containers, sauces, toppings, drinks and serving supplies.

5Transport

Use multiple cars for hot food, cold food and drinks/equipment.

6Market Day

Set up the assembly line, sell from 1:00 pm, track inventory and use markdowns only if required.

RISK MANAGEMENT

Plan for problems before they happen

HIGH IMPACT

Temperature control failure

Use insulated hot-food containers, coolers and ice packs. Monitor storage and minimise time outside safe storage.

MEDIUM IMPACT

Transport delay

Use multiple cars, split essential stock between vehicles and arrange later meat delivery if approved.

MEDIUM IMPACT

Slow sales

Increase promotion, push meal value, sell sides separately and use controlled late-day markdowns.

MEDIUM IMPACT

Unexpectedly high demand

Maintain strict 90 g portions, track remaining stock and keep 450 drinks available after food sells out.

Important planning assumptions
  • Ingredient costs are estimates until suppliers and receipts are confirmed.
  • The business assumes access to a smoker and multiple cars.
  • Later delivery of cooked meat is subject to teacher approval.
  • Food safety and temperature controls must follow the College’s Market Day requirements.
FOOD SAFETY & WHS

Built into the workflow

Food contamination

Gloves, tongs, clean surfaces and separate utensils.

Cold food warming

Ice boxes / coolers with ice packs.

Cash contamination

Cash handler does not directly handle ready-to-eat food.

Spills & crowding

Clean immediately and maintain a clear queue / collection area.

Heavy coolers

Use two-person lifting where required.

Allergens

Keep ingredient information available for customers.

SUSTAINABILITY

Reduce waste, keep value

Bulk purchasing

Reduce unnecessary packaging and lower unit cost.

Pre-portioned food

Reduce over-serving and improve consistency.

Late-day markdowns

Reduce leftover perishable stock.

Can recycling

Provide a clearly marked collection bin near the stall.
